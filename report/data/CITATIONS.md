## Deliberately not asserted

* The CEGMem prompt-priming figure (a class emitted 5.4% → 14.7% once named)
  and the bucket-coherence figures are from this project's own earlier
  measurements, not from the literature, and are described as such.
* No claim is made about the *wall-clock* or *monetary* cost of any cited
  system beyond what that system reports.
